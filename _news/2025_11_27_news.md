Made a Sticker Machine!

---

A cardboard sticker dispenser for Pokemon Go community meetups cause my usual method of just holding out a sandwich baggie was not cool enough. 

<a href="https://youtu.be/xEgJSdlHyD0?si=34RvgJQqoj_PbDtW"> Source </a>

I want to make a second one. I changed the measurements of the original to be smaller since the stickers are only about a square inch and I'd be carrying it around all day. However, the area between the turning tube and the dispense hole is too large. It still works, just not as smoothly as I'd like. 

Next up, a gachapon machine for Mega Stone pins! My deadline is the Kalos Tour

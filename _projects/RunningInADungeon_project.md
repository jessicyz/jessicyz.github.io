Running (in) a Dungeon was a game developed with Luting Wang, Everett Frank, and Sandy Chen. You play as a former office drone who wins their own dungeon. Now you are your own boss! 


The harder the dungeon room, the more adventurers you kill, and the more bodies you can loot. But be warned, you have to do your own dungeon maintenance and revivals aren't cheap! 

Made in Unity

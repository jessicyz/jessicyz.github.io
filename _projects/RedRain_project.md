<i>"Red Rain is a single-player, 3rd-person action-RPG set in the ruins of a post-Yellowstone supereruption North America. Protagonist Allie must raise and protect her only daughter Mya from a poisoned earth, ravenous wolves, and other survivors.<a>The game features real-time combat, a story-rich narrative told through Allie and her daughter's eyes, and a world where players can explore the devastated yet beautiful landscape of what was once known as Montana."

An engineer for Red Rain, in collaboration with Small Loan Studio. Built using Solarus Lua
